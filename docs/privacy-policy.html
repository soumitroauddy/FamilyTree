Privacy Policy

Family Tree App — com.skunkworks.family_tree_app

Effective July 1, 2026

👋 Overview

Family Tree App ("we," "us," or "our") is a cross-platform mobile and web application that helps families build and share interactive family trees. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

By creating an account or using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you disagree, please do not use the app.

📋 Information We Collect

Account & Identity Data

  • Email address (required for registration and password recovery)
  • Display name (chosen by you)
  • Authentication provider (email/password, Google, or Facebook)
  • OAuth provider user ID (when signing in via Google or Facebook)

Family Tree Data

  • Family name and join code you create
  • Family member profiles you add: full name, birth year, death year, biography, location, and relationships between members
  • Photos you upload for family members
  • Invitation codes (and optionally an invitee email address)

Device & Usage Data

  • Authentication session tokens (stored locally via shared_preferences)
  • Standard server request logs (IP address, timestamp, request path) — retained for up to 30 days
We do not collect precise device location, contacts, call logs, microphone audio, or any data beyond what is listed above.

⚙️ How We Use Your Information

Purpose Data Used
Create and authenticate your account Email, display name, OAuth tokens
Build and display your family tree Member profiles, photos, relationships
Send password-reset OTP codes Email address
Enable family sharing via invite codes Join codes, optional invitee email
Export your family tree data All family tree data (on request)
Debug errors and improve the service Anonymised server logs

Family Tree App relies on the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy that governs how they handle your data.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

🗄️ Data Storage & Security

  • Account and family tree data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted via Supabase.
  • Photos are stored in S3-compatible object storage with access restricted to authenticated users of your family.
  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Session tokens are persisted locally on your device using shared_preferences and are not shared with any third party.
  • We apply reasonable organisational and technical safeguards; however, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

🕐 Data Retention

  • Your account and family tree data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Server request logs are retained for up to 30 days and then deleted.
  • When you delete your account, all associated user records and uploaded photos are permanently removed. See Your Rights below.

✅ Your Rights & Choices

  • Access: You can view all personal data in the app at any time.
  • Correction: Update your display name and family member information directly in the app.
  • Export: Use the "Export Tree" feature to download all your family tree data as JSON.
  • Deletion: Delete your account from the app's account settings. This permanently removes your user record and all photos you uploaded. Family tree data you contributed to a shared family may be retained if other members remain active.
  • OAuth revocation: If you signed in with Google or Facebook, you may revoke access at any time through your Google Account or Facebook App settings.
To request manual data deletion or export, email us at the address listed in the Contact Us section below.

👶 Children's Privacy

Family Tree App is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Family members of any age may be recorded in the tree by an adult account holder, but no account may be created for a minor.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective" date at the top of the page will reflect the most recent revision. For material changes, we will notify you via an in-app notice or email before the change takes effect.

Continued use of the app after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
